# Safety and disclaimer

Roaming turns body movement into game input, which means people move around while they look at a screen. That can lead to falls, collisions and injuries, so the app asks every player to accept a safety warning before tracking starts and keeps reminding them while they play.

## Using Roaming safely

- **Only use Roaming in a safe environment**: a clear, flat area with enough space around you, away from traffic, stairs, open water, furniture, pets and other people.
- **Keep track of your surroundings at all times.**
- Stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath, and check with a doctor before starting a new exercise routine.
- Make sure the phone is mounted or held securely so it can't fall.

## In the app

- **First launch**: Roaming shows the safety warning and disclaimer, and it must be accepted before tracking can start. Choosing **Exit** closes the app. If the disclaimer changes in a later version, it is shown again.
- **Any time later**: the **Safety and disclaimer** button on the main screen shows the full text again.
- **Surroundings reminder**: while tracking, the motion bubble turns amber and shows "⚠ Watch your surroundings" for a few seconds, once when tracking starts and then regularly. The interval is set on the main screen (every 2, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, default 5) and can be changed while tracking. Reminders can't be turned off. They are shown on the bubble, so allow "display over other apps" to see them.

### For game developers

If your game is controlled through Roaming, please remind players about their surroundings too, for example on your start screen or pause menu, and avoid designs that push players to move fast in a small space.
